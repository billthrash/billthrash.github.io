Vision

Customer Orbit brings every customer conversation, signal, and success plan into one workspace so your team can focus on delivering value—not juggling tools.

Platform Capabilities

  • Centralize customer health, renewal risk, and expansion signals in customizable dashboards.
  • Automate playbooks across onboarding, adoption, QBRs, and renewal motions.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams using shared workspaces, notes, and accountability trackers.

Integrations

Connect Customer Orbit to the systems your team already lives in.

  • Google Workspace: Sync Calendar events, Gmail conversations, and Drive assets directly to customer timelines.
  • Microsoft 365: Align meetings, Teams collaboration, and shared documents with your customer success motions.
  • Salesforce: Bi-directional updates for accounts, opportunities, and support cases keep revenue teams and CSMs aligned.

Interaction Management

Plan, track, and analyze every touchpoint between your company and customers.

  • Timeline view of meetings, emails, and product usage milestones in a single place.
  • Shared success plans with tasks, owners, and due dates to drive accountability.
  • Sentiment analysis and call summaries to surface risks and expansion signals instantly.
